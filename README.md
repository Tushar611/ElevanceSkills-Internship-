# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard using Google Looker Studio

A Business Intelligence project developed during my Data Analytics Internship. This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using **Google Looker Studio** to generate interactive dashboards and business insights.

The project focuses on revenue trends, payment methods, average order value, discount impact, product performance, and weekday vs weekend sales analysis.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visualizations that help understand business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis includes:

- Monthly Revenue Trend (2022)
- Sales by Payment Method
- Average Order Value (AOV) Trend
- Revenue vs Discount Impact by Category
- Products with Largest Decrease in Sales (2021 vs 2022)
- Weekend vs Weekday Sales Analysis (Q4 2022)

---

# 📈 Dashboard Tasks

## 1️⃣ Monthly Revenue Trend (2022)

### Objective
Analyze monthly revenue performance throughout 2022.

### Visualizations
- Revenue Scorecard
- Total Orders
- Quantity Ordered
- Monthly Revenue Area Chart

### Insights
- Revenue peaked during August and September.
- Significant decline observed during Q4.
- Total Revenue exceeded **₹5 Billion**.

---

## 2️⃣ Sales by Payment Method

### Objective
Identify the most preferred customer payment methods.

### Visualizations
- Total Sales Scorecard
- Quantity Sold Scorecard
- Payment Method Summary Table

### Insights
- Cash on Delivery (COD) generated the highest sales.
- Digital payment methods contributed comparatively less.

---

## 3️⃣ Average Order Value (AOV) Trend

### Objective
Track monthly changes in Average Order Value.

### Visualizations
- Average Order Value Scorecard
- Monthly Area Line Chart

### Insights
- Highest AOV observed during August and September.
- Significant drop after September.

---

## 4️⃣ Revenue vs Discount Impact by Category

### Objective
Measure the impact of discounts across product categories.

### Visualizations
- Revenue Before Discount
- Revenue After Discount
- Discount Impact Scorecards
- Clustered Column Chart

### Insights
- Mobile & Tablets generated the highest revenue.
- Overall discount impact was relatively small.

---

## 5️⃣ Products with Largest Decrease in Sales (2021 vs 2022)

### Objective
Identify products with the highest decline in sales quantity.

### Methodology

- Aggregated sales by SKU for 2021
- Aggregated sales by SKU for 2022
- Calculated:

Decrease = Sales (2021) − Sales (2022)

- Selected Top 10 products with the largest decrease

### Visualization

- Horizontal Bar Chart

### Insights

- Several products experienced substantial sales declines.
- Useful for inventory planning and marketing strategies.

---

## 6️⃣ Weekend vs Weekday Sales (Q4 2022)

### Objective
Compare weekday and weekend sales performance during Q4 2022.

### Visualizations

- Overall Average Daily Sales
- Monthly Weekday vs Weekend Comparison

### Insights

- Weekday sales generally outperformed weekend sales.
- December showed stronger weekend purchasing behaviour.

---

# 📊 Business Insights

- Revenue crossed **₹5 Billion** in 2022.
- August and September were the strongest sales months.
- COD remained the most popular payment method.
- Mobile & Tablets contributed the highest revenue.
- Discounts had limited impact on overall revenue.
- Several products experienced declining demand between 2021 and 2022.
- Weekday sales were consistently higher than weekend sales.
